TIP 4: County jail food contract steered to favored vendor
CHANNEL: encrypted email
RECEIVED: 01 May 2026

I am a budget specialist in the Marlow County Sheriff's Office. The 2025 jail food-service contract, solicitation MCSO-FS-25-04, should have gone to Northfield Institutional Foods. Northfield scored higher and was $610,000 cheaper over three years than the incumbent, Carver Dining Group.

After bids closed on 11 February 2025, Undersheriff Nolan Brix asked me to replace the cost-weighting sheet with a version that gave "transition continuity" 25 percent of the score. That category was not in the solicitation. The revised sheet pushed Carver ahead by 3.4 points.

I have the original procurement packet, the revised workbook saved under Brix's network account, and a text message from Carver lobbyist Tess Rowland thanking Brix for "keeping the kitchen stable." The county board approved the $5.2 million award on 03 March 2025.

The revised scoring also removed a finding from the site-visit notes that Carver had failed two surprise sanitation checks in 2024. The finding was present in the PDF I reviewed on 13 February and absent from the version attached to the board packet on 27 February. Northfield's lower price was described to the board as "not reliably comparable" because of transition risk, but the file contains no written transition analysis.

I raised a records-retention concern to County Counsel's office on 04 March after the award, because the procurement folder showed two different versions of the same evaluation memo with the same file name. Counsel's paralegal told me by email that the final packet was the only official record. I kept screenshots of the version history before access was restricted.

TIP 2: Internally consistent two-year procurement scheme
CHANNEL: encrypted email
RECEIVED: 02 May 2026

I worked as procurement coordinator for Briar Port Authority from 03 January 2024 through 30 April 2026. This report concerns three maintenance contracts awarded to Seaward Mechanical during my tenure.

Contract BPA-M-2407 for pump repair closed on 12 July 2024 and was awarded on 29 July 2024 for $840,000. Contract BPA-M-2511 for dockside valve replacement closed on 06 March 2025 and was awarded on 21 March 2025 for $1,180,000. Contract BPA-M-2602 for winterization work closed on 09 January 2026 and was awarded on 24 January 2026 for $620,000.

In each file, Deputy Director Wallace Frayne added an "emergency readiness" score after bid close. The added points moved Seaward from second to first. I have the original score sheets, the revised sheets, and Frayne's emails to evaluator Mina Sol dated 26 July 2024, 19 March 2025, and 22 January 2026. The total value of the three awards is $2,640,000.

The pattern is visible in the file history. The original score sheets were saved before the evaluation meetings, while the revised sheets were saved one or two days before each award memo. In all three procurements, Seaward's price was higher than the first-ranked bidder before the emergency-readiness category was added. The procurement manual dated 01 May 2023 requires any scoring change after bid close to be approved by the general counsel, and I found no such approval in any of the three folders.

The pattern is consistent. A report is prepared by staff, then a manager asks for changes that make the results look better. The changed version is sent outside the company, while the original version stays in an internal folder. This has happened more than once and several people have seen it.

I do not have permission to share files right now. I am writing because someone outside the company should know that the official reports are not the same as the internal ones.

I understand this may be too vague to act on. I am leaving out details deliberately because I still work there and the reports are handled by a very small team. The issue is not a one-time typo or a formatting difference. It is a repeated practice where internal numbers are softened before they are sent to people outside the company.

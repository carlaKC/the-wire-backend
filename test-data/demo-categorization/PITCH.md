# Pitch — Demo 1: Categorization at Scale

## What you're showing

Ten anonymous tips, each written in a different voice, from different
reporters in different roles, about different programs and mechanisms.
The defense tips all describe one prime contractor; the pharma tips all
describe one sponsor. The system reads all ten and decides — on its own —
that they describe two distinct subject areas, and assigns each document
to the right one.

## What to say

> "Imagine you run a tip line. On a given week you might receive a hundred
> documents like these, in ten different voices, from a dozen different
> reporters. Today, an investigative editor has to read all of them just
> to figure out *what kinds of stories are in this stack*. Watch what
> happens here.
>
> [Submit case. Wait for processing.]
>
> The system has read all ten and split them into two topics. On the left
> — five tips about a single defense contractor. A subcontracts manager,
> a cost accountant, a subcontract administrator, a project manager, a
> travel coordinator. Different programs, different contract numbers,
> different fraud mechanisms. The system saw past all of that and
> recognized: this is one investigative beat.
>
> On the right — five tips about pharmaceutical data integrity at a
> single diversified sponsor. Clinical trial adverse event recoding,
> preclinical tox data exclusion, stability test manipulation,
> bioequivalence study fraud, post-market adverse event under-reporting.
> Different stages of the drug lifecycle, different products, different
> divisions. Same beat.
>
> No one told the system there were two topics. No one told it what to
> look for. It read the documents."

## Why this matters for investigators

This is the unlock for tip lines that today drown in volume. A small
investigative team can't read every submission. Most legitimate tips are
never reached. The system does the first-pass sort so editors can spend
their time on the documents that matter for the stories they're already
working — and surfaces the patterns across documents that no individual
reader would have seen.

TIP 2: Warehouse overtime was stripped from people who earned it
CHANNEL: intake form
RECEIVED: 07 May 2026

I supervise outbound loading at Harborline Logistics in Edison. I am furious writing this because I watched people work nights away from their kids and then saw the company erase the hours. This is not a misunderstanding. It is shameful.

On 19 February 2026 I watched Operations Manager Caleb Orr tell two leads to change the Kronos punches for the late trailer crew from 11.7 hours to 10.0 hours. The crew had stayed because trailer HLX-4028 arrived late from Elizabeth. Those workers did the work. The next morning their timecards showed a manager correction instead of the actual clock-out time.

I have screenshots from Kronos for employees 4412, 4480, and 4521, plus the dock log showing trailer HLX-4028 arrived at 9:42 PM. People cried in the break room about missing rent money. I cannot sleep knowing I signed off on a shift summary while this happened.

This was not a one-night problem. I saw the same thing after late trailers on 05 February and 12 March. Every time, the explanation from management was that "approved overtime" meant only overtime approved before the shift, even when the delay was caused by Harborline's own dispatch schedule. That is not how we explained overtime to the crew when we asked them to stay.

I am angry because the people affected are the lowest-paid employees in the building, but I am giving you the records that can be checked. The trailer logs, badge swipes, and Kronos correction history all line up. I will speak with an investigator, but I do not want my name given to Orr or Pike while I still supervise the floor.

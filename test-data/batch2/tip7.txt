CHANNEL: Signal message

أخي فادي

I left Tal al-Zaytoun three months ago. I worked there as senior planner. I am writing because I cannot stay silent.

The corruption at this project comes from one place — the office of the Deputy CEO, Bashar Hijazi. Everyone inside knows. The pattern is well established and has been operating for at least three years.

The mechanism involves a fixer in Amman called Ramzi Atwan who handles the payments and the placements. Hijazi controls which contractors win, which valuations are approved, which transfers go through. His personal share over the project's life so far, based on what I have seen and heard, is in the range of USD 2,400,000 — possibly more.

The project's CEO is largely ceremonial. The board does not look beyond the headline numbers. Hijazi is the operational power and the operational corruption.

I have weighed this carefully. I know the risks. But I also know that without external pressure nothing will change.

I have memos, internal calendar entries showing meetings with Atwan, and an email chain in which Hijazi instructs the procurement office to "process at the agreed structure." These I retained on personal devices.

I am willing to share when we have a channel agreed. Please keep my identity protected — Hijazi has reach inside our community.

May Allah strengthen you.

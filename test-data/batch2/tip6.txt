TIP 6: Land acquisition fraud against Beit Reema families
CHANNEL: secure drop
RECEIVED: 07 February 2026

I am writing on behalf of three families from the village of Beit Reema whose lands were acquired for the Tal al-Zaytoun project under what we now believe were fraudulent valuations.

The lands in question — totalling approximately 47 dunams — were acquired between 11 May 2022 and 22 September 2022 at an aggregate price of USD 2,820,000, paid in three tranches via bank transfer. We accepted the valuation at the time on the basis of comparables presented to us by an intermediary named Ramzi Atwan, who represented himself as acting on behalf of the project.

We have since come into possession, through a relative who works in the Ramallah land registry, of the appraisal report that the project itself filed for these same parcels. That report values our lands at USD 6,750,000 — more than double what we received. The differential of approximately USD 3,930,000 represents value that was extracted from our families and, we believe, retained by individuals inside the project.

The signature on our acquisition file is that of Bashar Hijazi, the Deputy CEO of Tal al-Zaytoun Development Holdings. His office handled the entire negotiation. We have weighed our options carefully and have decided to bring this to your attention because we do not believe Palestinian institutions will protect us against a project of this scale.

We are prepared to share the acquisition documents, the registry appraisal, and our identification under appropriate protections. We are seven adults representing the three families. We have not yet retained legal counsel.

نسأل الله أن يهديكم لما فيه الخير.

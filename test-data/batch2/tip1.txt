I write as a former Contracts Manager for Tal al-Zaytoun Development Holdings Limited, a position I held from 02 February 2022 until my resignation on 14 November 2024. I have weighed this carefully and I cannot in good conscience remain silent about what I observed.

The Deputy Chief Executive Officer, Bashar Hijazi, established and maintained over the period of my employment a system of bid rigging that benefitted three preferred contractors and his own private interests. The mechanism was the following:

— Tender specifications were drafted by Hijazi's office in coordination with a Jordanian intermediary based in Amman, Ramzi Atwan, who relayed them to the favoured bidders before publication.
— Favoured bidders submitted compliant proposals at pre-agreed prices that contained margins between 18 and 24 percent above market rates.
— The price differential was distributed: a portion to Atwan as facilitation fee, a portion repatriated to Hijazi via a Cyprus-registered entity called Kavala Trading Limited.

The total volume of contracts processed under this system during my tenure, by my reconstruction of the procurement files, was USD 41,300,000. The estimated personal benefit accruing to Hijazi over the same period was approximately USD 2,400,000.

I retained, before my departure, copies of fourteen tender award files including the marked technical evaluations and the corresponding correspondence with Atwan. I did not retain originals. The copies are with me. I am willing to provide them through whatever channel you specify.

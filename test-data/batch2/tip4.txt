TIP 4: Subcontractor's account of forced "facilitation" payments
CHANNEL: postal mail (translated from Arabic)
RECEIVED: 06 February 2026

To Mr. Fadi Elsalameen, after greetings:

I write you these words from one who has lost much and gained little in his dealings with Tal al-Zaytoun Development. I am the owner of a small construction subcontracting company that worked on three of the project's residential phases between 2022 and 2024. I have weighed this carefully before writing.

The story is brief but instructive. Our company submitted competitive bids for fourteen tender packages over those years. We were awarded three. In each of the three, after award, we were informed that we were required to engage a "facilitation consultant" — a Jordanian named Ramzi Atwan — at a cost equivalent to 12 percent of the contract value. This was non-negotiable. We complied because we needed the work.

The total we paid to Atwan over the three contracts was USD 580,000. We have receipts for these payments, billed as "site logistics consulting" through a Jordanian company associated with him.

The eleven tenders we bid on and lost — all of them — were awarded to one of three contractors I will name to you privately. We have come to understand that those three contractors had similar arrangements but at higher rates and with the resulting price loaded into the tender amounts approved by the Deputy CEO, Mr. Bashar Hijazi.

I have spoken with two other subcontractors who can attest to similar experiences. They are willing to come forward but want to see how my own approach to you is received first.

I am sending this through the post because the digital channels are not safe in our country.

May Allah preserve you and grant you success in your work.
